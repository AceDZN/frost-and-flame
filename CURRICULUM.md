## Corrections and qualifications kept from the handoff

- **Mass is conserved in every phase change.** Bodies change phase by enthalpy; mass moves between bodies only through explicit transfers (melt water running off, steam rising into the balloon, condensate dripping). Tests check total water mass in every level.
- **Water expands slightly when it freezes**: basins draw ice at 917 kg/m³ and water at 1000 kg/m³; it is never shown as a mass change.
- **Steam**: the gas (אדי מים) is transparent; white clouds are condensed droplets. The balloon's size follows the real volume of 100 °C steam (0.6 kg/m³), capped visually.
- **Units and scale.** Masses follow object volumes; energies are in kJ (HUD in MJ) and obey Q = m·c·ΔT and Q = m·L with textbook values (water c = 2.1 / 4.18 / 2.0 kJ/kg·°C, L_f = 334, L_v = 2260 kJ/kg; CO₂ sublimation −78.5 °C, 571 kJ/kg). The gloves are deliberately very strong (100 MJ/s) so puzzles finish in seconds. Conduction through materials is sped up by one shared factor (×30,000), so the ratios between copper, iron, wood and air stay real. The sun mirror's power is game-scaled. A frozen creek "bridge" models only the top water layer of a flowing creek.
- **The gloves are a heat battery, not magic**: the cold glove moves heat out of a body into the battery, and the heat glove spends it. The cold glove only cools things down to the surrounding air temperature.
- Temperatures are in °C with water's 0 and 100 at normal pressure.

## Learning events

`Game.learn` records anonymous local events (level, action, target, value, whether hints were visible, retry count): glove starts per target, phase-change starts/finishes, glass blocks, splashes, plunger results, battery empty, hint shown, win. They are saved to `localStorage` key `frost-flame-learning-v1` only. Levels 1–2 always show hints, 3–5 show a hint after 9 s on a step, and 6 shows none, so first unaided decisions can be told apart from hinted ones.
